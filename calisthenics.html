GYM20MUSIC provides powerful calisthenics music for training, workouts, strength, focus and motivation.

Calisthenics is a bodyweight training discipline built around strength, control, movement and performance. The right music can help create a focused and energetic training environment.

This GYM20MUSIC collection is designed around workout music, gym music, focus music, energy and motivation for athletes and people who enjoy calisthenics and physical training.

GYM20MUSIC is created and curated by Behrooz Khanahmadloo (بهروز خان احمدلو). Learn more on the creator profile page.

For bodyweight training, choose music that fits the session: steady rhythms can support controlled skill practice, while higher-energy tracks may suit intense workout sets. Keep the volume at a level that lets you stay aware of your surroundings.
